Appraisal: Cards & Gems — Request Deletion of Your Data

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Azure Sky Studio Dev ("we," "us," or "our") deletes, on request, the information we hold about you in connection with Appraisal: Cards & Gems (the "Game"). The Game does not use accounts. The only information we hold about players comes from online play.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to azureskystudiodev@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the name you used for online play and, if available, the room code of each online game. If you do not have the room code, include the approximate date of the game and the names of the other players.
  3. We may ask for further details of the game to locate the room and to confirm that the information is yours.
  4. We will reply by email when the deletion is complete.

Information that is deleted

We delete each online room identified in your request, including all information in it:

  • the name and avatar choice you used for online play;
  • the one-way fingerprint of your device identifier;
  • your moves and the game state, including cards, scores, and game options;
  • the times you were last connected.

A room is deleted as a whole. If its game is still in progress, deleting the room ends the game for all players in it.

Information that is kept

  • Copies held by our hosting provider: copies of a deleted room may remain in our hosting provider's backups and technical logs for a limited time, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • IP address fingerprint: the one-way fingerprint of your IP address that our server keeps when you create a room is deleted automatically after about ten minutes.
  • Your request: we keep your email and our reply as a record of the request.
  • Information on your device: your settings, saved game, and statistics are stored on your device and are not held by us. You can delete them by uninstalling the Game or by clearing its data in your device's settings.
  • Advertising information: information collected by Google's advertising software is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Google describes its use of this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Automatic deletion

Online rooms are also deleted automatically after use, without a request, as described under "Retention of online information" in our Privacy Policy at https://azureskystudiodev.github.io/appraisal/privacy.html.
