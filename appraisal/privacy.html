Appraisal: Cards & Gems — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Azure Sky Studio Dev ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information in connection with Appraisal: Cards & Gems (the "Game"), a free card game for Android that is supported by advertising. The Game can be played entirely on your device or online with other players.

Information stored on your device

The Game stores the following information on your device:

  • your settings, including sound, music, vibration, animation, and avatar preferences, and the name you use for online play;
  • your saved game and your statistics;
  • the choices from your last new game, including any names entered for players sharing the device;
  • your progress through the tips of the guided first game;
  • if you play online, a randomly generated device identifier;
  • counts used to schedule full-screen ads: the number of games started and finished since the last full-screen ad, the time that ad was shown, and the number of times the Game has been opened.

Except as described under Online play, this information is not sent to us. Google's advertising software in the Game operates whenever your device is connected to the internet, including during single-player games, as described under Advertising.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this information in the backup it stores in your Google account and restore it when the Game is reinstalled or installed on another device. We do not have access to these backups. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes this information from your device.

Online play

Online play is optional. When you use online play, the Game sends the following information to our server:

The name you enter for online play, and your avatar choiceTo identify you to the other players in the roomThe other players in the room

After you have used online play, the Game also contacts the server each time the main menu opens, to show how many of your online games are waiting for your move. These requests include your device identifier, name, and avatar choice.

Reactions are preset messages. The server passes them to the other players in the room and does not store them. The Game has no free-text chat.

The Game does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, or location, and does not require an account. We do not sell information from online play or use it for advertising. We disclose it only to the other players in your room, as described above, and to our hosting provider, which operates the server on our behalf.

Retention of online information

An online room, including all information in it, is deleted automatically. Deletion runs every ten minutes, so a room is deleted within about ten minutes of any of the following:

  • when all players have left the room;
  • if its game has not started, one hour after the room was last open on any player's device, and in any case three hours after the room was created;
  • one day after the room was last used, if its game has finished;
  • seven days after the room was last used, if its game is in progress.

If the online service is paused because it has not been used for some time, deletion takes place when it resumes. If you leave a game while other players continue, your name and moves remain in the room until the room is deleted. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's backups and technical logs for a limited time after deletion.

Hosting and security

The online server is operated by a cloud hosting provider in the United States. The provider receives your device's IP address when the Game connects to the server and may keep it for a limited time in technical logs. When you create a room, our server also keeps a one-way fingerprint of the IP address, which cannot be turned back into the address, for about ten minutes, to limit how many new rooms can be created from one connection. Information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Advertising

The Game displays ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the screen, including during play, and, from time to time, a full-screen ad between games, when a game ends or a new game begins. Full-screen ads are not shown during play. Ad content is limited to material rated G or PG.

Google's advertising software in the Game collects information from your device and sends it to Google, including your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, information about your device and the Game, and your interactions with ads. Google uses this information to select, display, and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information. AdMob provides us only with aggregate reports, such as the number of ads shown and the resulting earnings. Google describes its use of this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law, Google asks for your consent before personalizing ads, and the Game's Settings include Ad privacy choices, where you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings, typically under Google > Ads or Privacy > Ads.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through online play, please contact us, and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the address below. Requests should include the room code, if available. Instructions for requesting deletion, and the information that is deleted and kept, are available at https://azureskystudiodev.github.io/appraisal/delete-data.html. Some U.S. states provide additional privacy rights, and we will respond to requests as required by applicable law.
