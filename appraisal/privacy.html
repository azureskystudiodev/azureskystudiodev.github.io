Appraisal: Cards & Gems — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Appraisal: Cards & Gems ("Appraisal", "the game") is a free card game with ads, made by Azure Sky Studio Dev ("we", "us"). You can play it entirely on your device, or online with friends. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

Playing on your device

Single-player games and pass-and-play games never leave your phone. The game keeps these on your device only:

  • your settings (sound, vibration, animations, avatar, and the name you use online)
  • your saved game and your statistics
  • for online play: a random code that identifies this device, and the codes of the online rooms you've joined

Nothing here is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game (or clearing its data) deletes it.

Playing online

Online play is optional. When you create or join a room, the game sends our server:

WhatWhyWho sees it
The name you type for online play, and your avatar choiceSo the other players know who you areThe other players in your room
A random device code (made on your phone; it isn't your phone's serial number or advertising ID)So you can come back to your seat after closing the gameOnly the server. It stores a scrambled fingerprint of the code, never the code itself
Your moves, and the game itself (cards, scores, options)To run the game fairly, so nobody can see anyone else's cardsThe other players see your moves as the rules allow; your hand is only ever sent to you
When you were last connectedSo the AI can play your turn if you leaveOnly the server

Reactions (the preset messages like "Nice!") pass through our server to the other players in your room and aren't stored. There's no free-text chat.

We don't ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any account. We don't sell or share this information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long it's kept

A room, and everything in it, is deleted automatically:

  • as soon as everyone has left it (you can leave a game at any time, from the game or from your list of games);
  • an hour after anyone was last in it, if the game never started;
  • a day after it was last used, if the game has finished;
  • 7 days after the last move, for a game in progress.

Where it's kept

The online server is run on Supabase (supabase.com), a hosting service, in the United States. Like any internet service, Supabase sees your connection's IP address when your phone talks to it, and may keep it briefly in its technical logs. Information travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner along the top of the screen, and now and then a full-screen ad at a break between games: as a game ends, or as you start a new one (never during play). Ads are limited to content suitable for most audiences.

To show ads, Google's ad code in the app collects information on your device and sends it to Google: your phone's advertising ID, your connection's IP address (and the rough location it shows), basic information about the phone and the app, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses it to choose, show, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. We don't receive any of this information ourselves. How Google uses it is explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google asks for your consent before personalizing ads, the first time you open the game. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).

The game keeps a few small counts on your device (how many games you've started and finished since the last full-screen ad, when it showed, and how many times the game has been opened), so full-screen ads stay occasional and none show the first time you play. These never leave your device.

Children

Appraisal is a card game for everyone, made for players aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Nothing in the game is unsuitable for children, and whether a child plays it is up to their parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information through online play, contact us and we'll delete it. (Rooms delete themselves within 7 days anyway.)
